Good next additions, in order

Personalized discovery filters: Let people filter by skills, interests, location, or “looking for” (cofounder, open-source work, study buddy). Skills and interests already exist on profiles; “looking for” would be a small profile/schema addition.
A better first hello: Add optional intro prompts to requests, such as “What would you like to build together?” That gives the recipient something to respond to when reviewing a request.
Messaging after connecting: Give accepted connections a private conversation, starting with simple text messages. This is probably the biggest product upgrade; it needs message storage and endpoints, with access limited to the two connected users. Real-time updates can come later.
Collaboration profiles: Add availability, preferred roles, current project ideas, and links to GitHub/portfolio. This helps people judge whether they’re a good fit beyond matching on skills.
Match context: Explain why someone appeared: “You both know React” or “You’re both interested in open source.” That makes discovery feel more intentional.
Undo and saved profiles: Let users revisit a recent pass or bookmark someone to consider later. These are satisfying quality-of-life additions to the swipe flow.
Trust and safety controls: Add block/report, profile visibility controls, and account deletion. Especially important before adding messaging.
For a manageable first milestone, I’d build intro messages on requests, then basic messaging for accepted connections. That creates a complete loop from discovery to an actual conversation.
